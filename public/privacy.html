Catapult Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

The short version

Catapult is a personal vision board and daily practice app. Your content stays on your device and, if you choose to connect it, in your own Google Drive. We operate no servers, collect no analytics, and never see your data.

What the app stores

Everything you create in Catapult (pages, images, audio, ratings, journal entries) is stored locally in your browser's storage on your own device.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off by default. If you connect Google Drive, the app requests the narrowest available permission (drive.file), which allows it to access only the files it creates itself: a private folder in your Drive holding your board, its media, and a journal spreadsheet. The app cannot see, read, or modify anything else in your Drive. Your data moves directly between your device and your Google account; it never passes through any server of ours, because none exists.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, and no accounts of its own. The only network connections it makes are to Google, on your instruction, for the sync you configured.

Data deletion

Your data is yours to delete: remove the app's site data on your device, and delete the app's folder from your own Google Drive. No copy exists anywhere else.

Google API Services disclosure

Catapult's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
